# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: the CS3383 teaching team and classmates who meet Sentinel through a shared link or a projected slide during the group presentation. Secondary: the Group 15 team itself, and later recruiters reading the team members' GitHub profiles. They arrive from a WhatsApp link on a phone or see the page projected in a lecture hall.

## Product Purpose

Sentinel is an agentic AI tool that audits a codebase for security vulnerabilities, recommends fixes a developer can read, refactors the affected code on its own branch, and verifies each patch with a re-scan and the tests before a person approves it. The landing page explains that loop in under a minute and shows one real finding from start to finish. Success: a visitor can retell what Sentinel does and why its fixes can be trusted.

## Positioning

Sentinel closes the loop that scanners and autofix tools leave open: every fix it proposes arrives with evidence that it works.

## Conversion & proof

- The primary action opens the deployed Sentinel workspace. Visitors can explore a sample audit and download a PDF report. The secondary action scrolls to the terminal workflow. The team repository link is in the footer.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Finds the security holes in your code, explains them, fixes them, and proves the fix."
- Belief ladder: AI-written code is often insecure; warnings alone do not fix anything; Sentinel fixes and proves; a human still decides.
- Proof on hand: Pearce et al., "Asleep at the Keyboard?" (about 40% of 1,689 Copilot programs vulnerable), from the CS3383 reading list. No product metrics exist yet, so none are shown.

## Brand Personality

Watchful, plain-spoken, exact. Calm confidence rather than alarm. Explains like a senior engineer in a code review.

## Anti-references

Hacker-green terminal pages, purple AI gradients, grids of identical feature cards, invented statistics, stock photos of hooded figures.

## Design Principles

- Show the evidence: every claim on the page is backed by a source or a concrete example.
- Human in charge: the design always ends with a person approving.
- Plain words over jargon; the code speaks where code is clearer.
- Readable from the back of a lecture hall.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Reduced-motion alternatives for every animation. Colour is never the only signal (diff lines carry + and - markers).
